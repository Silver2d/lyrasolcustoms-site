What We Build

LyrasolCustoms is a small, independent software studio. We make things we'd want on our own devices — built on a simple principle: your data is yours, and it should stay on your device unless you decide otherwise.

No surveillance, no data harvesting, no dark patterns. Just honest software that does what it says.

Our Apps
